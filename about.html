hello, I'm Keshia

I got my first point-and-shoot digital camera when I was eight years old and never looked back.

Something about finding the right lines in a frame and watching the light evolve behind a lens just tickles my brain. The same feeling I get from a shot of caffeine, a poignant turn of phrase, or solving a never-before-solved scientific problem (I am a Doctor of Philosophy). You can find pieces of me all over the internet, but my body is usually either on the little red dot known as Singapore or on a plane going back and forth home to Jakarta.
